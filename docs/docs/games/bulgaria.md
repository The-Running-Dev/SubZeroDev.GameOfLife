# Bulgaria — Source Material

**Status:** Shared source scenes. Neutral — used by two different games.

> The scenes below are **deadpan Bulgarian vignettes** — bureaucracy, inheritance,
> apartment politics, absurdity. They are creative source, committed to no mechanics.
> **Two different games** draw on them, each framing them for its own kind:
>
> - [`life-in-the-fast-lane.md`](life-in-the-fast-lane.md) — as a
>   **culture pack**: events, jobs and situations inside the weekly life-sim.
> - [`bulgaria-adventure.md`](bulgaria-adventure.md) — as **nodes** in a
>   branching make-your-own-adventure.
>
> The two share only this material and the deadpan tone (world absurd, narrator not —
> [`02-narrative-voice.md`](02-narrative-voice.md)). They share nothing
> mechanical. What "Wait" actually *does* is authored per game, not fixed here.

### Municipality

You arrive at the municipality at 08:03. The office opened at 08:00. A handwritten
note reads: *"Closed for a meeting until 11:30."*

Choices: Wait · Try another entrance · Ask the security guard · Go for coffee and
accidentally meet the mayor's cousin.

### Property Inheritance

Your aunt informs you she owns the entire property because she has maintained the
tomatoes since 1998.

Choices: Request the cadastral records · Call your mother · Consult a lawyer · Cut the
padlock · Accept that tomato maintenance apparently establishes ownership.

### Apartment Block

The neighbour has parked diagonally across three parking spaces. When questioned, he
responds: *"I've always parked like this."*

Choices: Leave a note · Knock on the door · Park behind him · Call the building
manager · Decide today's problem belongs to tomorrow.

### Government Office

You have collected every required document. The clerk carefully reviews your paperwork.
She smiles. One certificate is now older than three months. You must begin again.

Choices: Accept your fate · Ask to speak to someone else · Search for another office ·
Question reality.

### Starting a Business

Congratulations. Your company is officially registered. Your first visitors arrive: the
Tax Agency, the Labour Inspectorate, the Health Inspectorate. None of them are
customers.

Choices: Offer them coffee · Hide · Ask who invited them · Pretend the business never
opened.

### Driving

You pass the annual inspection. Five minutes later the dashboard lights up like a
Christmas tree. The mechanic confidently says: *"It was already like that."*

Choices: Believe him · Ask for another opinion · Ignore the warning · Turn up the
music.

### Village Life

You inherit a beautiful village house. Unfortunately it comes with: five co-owners, two
missing deeds, one disputed fence, and three generations of unresolved arguments.

Choices: Attempt mediation · Measure the land yourself · Ask the oldest neighbour ·
Pretend you never inherited it.

### Family Meeting

The family gathers to peacefully discuss inheritance. Within twelve minutes someone
references an argument from 1994. Nobody remembers why. Everyone remembers who started
it.

Choices: Stay silent · Change the subject · Bring out the documents · Leave before
lunch.

### Bureaucracy

The clerk sends you to Room 14. Room 14 sends you to Room 6. Room 6 informs you that
everything happens in Room 14.

Choices: Continue the cycle · Ask for a supervisor · Go home · Wonder if this is a side
quest.

### Entrepreneur

You successfully open your first business. Your first invoice is paid… in approximately
60 days. Your supplier expects payment tomorrow.

Choices: Call the client · Negotiate · Borrow money · Discover entrepreneurship.

### Expat Returns

After years abroad, you return to Bulgaria. Within the first week: someone asks why you
came back; someone offers unsolicited advice; someone tells you they could have done
what you did abroad; someone knows a cheaper mechanic.

Choices: Smile · Explain · Laugh · Accept your destiny.

### BMW Ownership

Your BMW develops a mysterious noise. Three mechanics diagnose: suspension,
transmission, "they all do that." A fourth mechanic fixes it with a hammer.

Choices: Pay immediately · Ask what he actually fixed · Never ask questions · Buy him
lunch.

### Ultimate Bulgarian Reward

Congratulations. After seven years of paperwork, you finally receive: €300, and 28
years of unresolved legal responsibility.

**Achievement Unlocked: "It Builds Character."**
