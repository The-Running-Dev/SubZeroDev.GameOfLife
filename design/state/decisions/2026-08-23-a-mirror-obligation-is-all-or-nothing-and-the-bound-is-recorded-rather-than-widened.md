Date: 2026-08-23

Status: accepted

## Claim
No amendment to the check and no new marker form. `20-contract.md` § *Authored records* now states that an obligation asserts its body is a complete restatement of the declaration's membership, that a site describing a subset is not a weak obligation but not an obligation, and that such a site is reduced per *Reduce the mirrored surface* (2026-08-20) or left to the full-audit path. The bound is written beside SS16's rather than under it: SS16 says the obligation set cannot be proven complete, this says even a complete set reaches only the exact restatements.
