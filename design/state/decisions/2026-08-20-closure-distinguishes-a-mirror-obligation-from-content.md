# decision/2026-08-20-closure-distinguishes-a-mirror-obligation-from-content
Date: 2026-08-20
Anchor: 2026-08-20 — Closure distinguishes a mirror obligation from content
Status: accepted

## Claim
Derive *closure* per declaration — fixed membership (interface, literal union, enum) versus content-supplied membership (keyed map or record). Only closed declarations can carry a mirror obligation. Closure is derived from the declaration's form, never declared.
